Star Chart Project · Observation Log 004

2026-10-30 · Observatory ASTRA-04 · Seeing 2/5

At 2:17 a.m., the telescope pointed at the third blank patch toward Cygnus. The first frame from the data stream had no stars, just a thin layer of noise, like static on an old TV.

"If three nights in a row are all noise, it means we looked in the wrong direction, not that the telescope is broken."

The control-room air conditioning was louder than usual. The duty roster listed four names, but only two people were actually there. The other two seats were taken by coffee cups and a laptop still running calibration.
